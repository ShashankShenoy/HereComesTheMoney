RBI / LEDGER FIXES - 15 OCTOBER 2026

The three functional failures from the audit have been corrected.

1. Treasury work-item creation
   TreasuryRepository now specifies the insert columns. Oracle supplies OPEN,
   OPENED_AT and VERSION_NO defaults instead of receiving explicit null values.
   Creation and independent checker decisions pass on Oracle with rollback
   fixtures. HTTP regression coverage verifies creation, defaults, self-approval
   rejection, stale-version rejection and use of the authenticated checker.

2. Journal balance-control inquiry
   Current employees with existing global GL_RECONCILE authority can read the
   full journal control result. Customer and ordinary transaction-viewer masking
   remains enforced. The inquiry exposes balanced status and debit/credit totals
   to admin and checker, and preserves the correct missing-journal error.

3. Account 6 synthetic opening funding
   The user confirmed that the pre-existing INR 500,000 was intended as synthetic
   demo opening funds. The missing opening funding is now recorded explicitly:
     Transaction 110, type OPENING_FUNDING, status POSTED.
     Journal 227, posting key demo-opening:account-6:20261015.
     Debit INR 500,000 to dedicated asset GL 35, DEMO_OPENING_FUNDS.
     Credit INR 500,000 to the account's existing customer-liability GL.
   The opening funding uses a dedicated synthetic asset. It does not represent
   a physical cash receipt or an RBI reserve movement.
   The existing unjournaled seed offset was rebased inside the locked transaction
   before the ledger service posted the journal. The final customer posted and
   spendable balances both remain INR 500,000; no duplicate credit occurred.
   Module 4's ordered projection was updated through its projection policy/store.
   Account position and ledger-derived balance are now both INR 500,000, with
   zero difference. Period-close approval now succeeds with a separate checker.

   Earlier in this session, the separately approved rebuild to zero committed.
   At the user's request, the previous INR 500,000 was restored immediately.
   Both operations remain audited. The final synthetic-funding correction above
   preserves INR 500,000 and leaves all previous journal/posting rows intact.
