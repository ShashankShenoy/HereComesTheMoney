Payments between two demo users are working

Verified on 11 October 2026. The running demo now has a direct account transfer in Payments, using the existing atomic ledger transfer service. This immediately debits the sender and credits the recipient, including when external payment simulation is disabled.

Sign in, open Payments → Send to another bank account, select your source account, enter the other user's account number and amount, and review and confirm. The recipient finds their account number in My accounts. Each user can refresh their own sent and received transfers.
