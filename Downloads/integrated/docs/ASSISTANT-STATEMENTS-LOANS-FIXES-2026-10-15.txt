AI ASSISTANT, STATEMENTS AND LOANS FIXES
15 October 2026

SCOPE AND RESULT
This repair addresses the four findings in
ASSISTANT-STATEMENTS-LOANS-CHECK-2026-10-15.txt.

1. CHECKER ASSISTANT ACCESS
Checker chat and MCP inquiry requests are permitted. Checker tools are restricted
to scoped account, position, transaction, customer-search and pending-beneficiary
inquiries. Draft/write tools and action confirmation remain denied. Local tests
use a stub model; they do not send data to the external AI provider.

2. LOAN SANCTION BOUNDS
Approval validates sanctioned amount and tenure against the selected product
version's minimum and maximum values, in addition to existing application and
checker-authority limits. Conversion repeats that validation so an old invalid
approval cannot create a facility. Existing Oracle facilities meet these bounds.

3. STAFF STATEMENTS
The approved repair created these six configuration records using administrator
drafts and a separate checker approval:
- One active M10_STATEMENTS purpose, STAFF_ACCOUNT_STATEMENT.
- One approved WEB / STAFF masking profile, STAFF_WEB_STATEMENTS.
- Four effective en-IN / STAFF narrations: NORMAL, REVERSAL, RETURN, ADJUSTMENT.
The staff form only offers active, currently effective statement-service purposes.
Authorization also checks the owning service. Narration discovery includes its
effective dates. The setup tool reuses suitable approved configuration on replay.
No new live statement was requested or issued.

4. PARTIAL AND FULL LOAN PREPAYMENT
Implemented a read-only prepayment quote and a separate servicing command for
PARTIAL and FORECLOSURE. Existing scheduled repayments retain their behavior.
The amount is the total debit, including interest through the payment date.

Partial payment covers scheduled dues, elapsed interest and additional principal.
It reduces future principal installments while preserving contractual due dates
and maturity. Full foreclosure requires the exact current payoff and closes the
facility. Product prepayment flags, borrower/account ownership, account status,
scope and servicing permissions are enforced. Fee/penalty balances that need a
separate allocation workflow are explicitly rejected. Current products use the
supported fixed-rate, monthly, single-disbursement reducing-balance configuration.

Posting, interest accrual, allocations, projections and schedule replacement are
atomic. Insufficient funds roll everything back. Concurrent identical requests
debit once; replay with changed payment terms is rejected. Historical journals
remain immutable. No schema migration or live loan transaction was required.

The browser form loads a quote, validates amounts and refreshes the quote before
submission. A changed payoff requires review and resubmission. Foreclosure
autofills and locks the visible Oracle JET amount field. Facility changes clear
the displayed amount; switching to PARTIAL restores editability.
