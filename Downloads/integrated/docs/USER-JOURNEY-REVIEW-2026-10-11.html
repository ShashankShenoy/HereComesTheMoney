The demo privacy journey passed with its synthetic policy fixture. No real customer money, consent or policy records were modified by the browser tests. Oracle was checked with read-only queries and application health checks.

Fixes delivered

  • Customer transfers now post using account numbers without requiring access to the recipient’s private account details.
  • Privacy forms supply the linked customer, approved notice verification and web channel automatically. Customers review the notice and choose allow or deny.
  • Completed statement requests retain their issued statement reference and offer direct PDF, CSV and HTML download buttons.
  • Refreshing a session reloads authorized navigation after an independent role grant.
  • Repeated till creation shows whether today’s till is already open or closed, before allocating more vault cash.
  • The local synthetic demo now includes a separately approved audit-retention fixture. Oracle policy data is unchanged.
